# National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS)

**Source:** National Clean Air Programme (NCAP) Report, Table 2, Page 11  
**Notified by:** Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) under the Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981  
**Notification Date:** November 2009  
**Category:** Authority — Standards Reference  

---

## Legal and Methodological Basis

- NAAQS are standards for ambient (outdoor) air quality notified by CPCB under the Air Act, 1981.
- **Annual standards** = annual arithmetic mean of a **minimum 104 measurements** in a year at a particular site, taken twice a week at 24-hourly intervals.
- **Short-term (24h/8h/1h) standards** shall be complied with **98% of the time** in a year. A 2% exceedance is permitted but **not on two consecutive days** of monitoring.

## Additional Regulatory Context

- MoEF&CC has notified environmental standards for **84 sectors** (effluent standards for 45 industrial sectors; emission standards for 63 industrial sectors).
- Installation of an **Online Continuous (24×7) Monitoring System (OCEMS)** is mandatory for **17 categories** of highly polluting industries.
- A **Non-Attainment City (NAC)** is a city where NAAQS is exceeded for any one notified parameter for **5 or more consecutive years** based on NAMP data, with a minimum of three monitoring stations reporting.
- As of 2019, **102 NACs** were identified, including all major Maharashtra cities: Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, Nashik, Aurangabad, Navi Mumbai, and 11 others.

## Monitoring Methodology (NAMP)

- **NAMP** = National Air Quality Monitoring Programme
- National network: 703 manual stations covering 307 cities/towns across 29 states and 6 UTs
- **CAAQMS**: 134 continuous real-time stations in 71 cities across 17 states
- **Pollutants monitored under NAMP**: SO₂, NO₂, PM₁₀, PM₂.₅
- **Pollutants monitored under CAAQMS**: PM₁₀, PM₂.₅, SO₂, NOx, NH₃, CO, O₃, Benzene
- Sampling frequency: 24 hours, twice a week = **104 observations per year**
- Meteorological parameters integrated: wind speed, wind direction, relative humidity, temperature
