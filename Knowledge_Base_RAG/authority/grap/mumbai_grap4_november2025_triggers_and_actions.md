## 1. Background: Mumbai GRAP Stage 4 Trigger — November 2025

Mumbai has historically relied on its coastal location and sea breeze to remain protected from severe winter smog seen in North India. However, data from the 4th week of November 2025 demonstrated that this protection is eroding.

**GRAP Stage 4 was triggered by BMC on Friday, November 28, 2025**, applied to the following areas:
- Mazgaon
- Malad
- Deonar
- Borivali (East)
- Chakala Andheri (East)
- Navy Nagar
- Mulund (West)
- Powai

These neighbourhoods had been showing consistent pollution spikes for multiple consecutive days prior to the GRAP 4 activation.

---

## 2. AQI Data That Triggered GRAP Stage 4

### AQI Measurements (November 24–28, 2025)
Three key monitoring locations — Chakala Andheri East, Malad, and Borivali East — all crossed **AQI 300** (Very Poor category) multiple times per day across the monitoring period.

**Key data points:**
- **Highest AQI peak: 342** (November 27, 5:00 AM) — reflecting trapped pollutants and strong overnight stagnation
- AQI stayed between **200–250 even after midnight**, indicating zero overnight dispersion
- Consistent double-peaked pollution pattern every day:
  - **Morning peaks:** 5:00 AM – 10:00 AM
  - **Evening peaks:** 6:00 PM – 11:00 PM

### Why These Peaks Occur
The synchronized morning and evening pollution surges reflect the combined effect of:
- Traffic emissions during rush hours
- Construction dust accumulation
- Stagnant winter air preventing dispersion
- Winter temperature inversion effect — trapping pollutants near ground level (similar to Delhi-NCR conditions)
- Nighttime AQI never returning to healthy levels — indicating a prolonged, multi-day pollution episode

**Definition under Mumbai GRAP:** A persistent AQI above 300 across multiple stations and multiple days constitutes the trigger condition for Stage 4 activation.

---

## 3. Mumbai GRAP Structure and Activation Thresholds

Mumbai's GRAP system is structured differently from Delhi's GRAP. In Mumbai's framework, **GRAP Stage 4 is described as the base/lowest level** of the Graded Response Action Plan (this reflects BMC's nomenclature, not a comparison to Delhi's Stage 4):

| Mumbai GRAP Stage | PM₂.₅ Trigger Level | PM₁₀ Trigger Level | Description |
|-------------------|--------------------|--------------------|-------------|
| GRAP-4 (Base) | 61–120 µg/m³ | 101–350 µg/m³ | Moderate to Poor Air Quality; early signs of pollution rise |

**Activation condition:** PM₂.₅ levels reaching 61–120 µg/m³ OR PM₁₀ reaching 101–350 µg/m³, sustained over multiple days and across multiple monitoring stations.

## 4. GRAP Stage 4 Restrictions and Authority Actions

When GRAP Stage 4 is activated in Mumbai, the following actions are implemented by authorities:

### Enforcement Actions
1. **Strictly enforce garbage-burning bans** in landfills and public spaces
2. **Ensure dust control at construction sites** (covering materials, wet suppression, site perimeter management)
3. **Tighten industrial emission rules** and close violating units immediately
4. **Deploy traffic management teams** to prevent congestion-related emission spikes
5. **Intensify PUC (Pollution Under Control) checks** for vehicles on roads
6. **Carry out mechanised road sweeping** and water sprinkling, including on unpaved roads

### Operational Enforcement Details
- Special flying squads comprising engineers, police teams, and GPS-enabled vehicles inspect every ward
- Violations at construction sites are issued notices immediately
- Out of 70 sites checked during one enforcement round, **53 were found violating dust-control rules** and issued notices

---

## 5. Impact of GRAP Stage 4 — Observed Results

After GRAP Stage 4 was introduced on Friday (November 28, 2025):
- AQI across all three monitoring locations (Andheri East, Malad, Borivali East) dropped sharply
- **Peak AQI of 342 at 5:00 AM fell to approximately 158 by 3:00 PM** — the cleanest point of the week
- This was the **first significant drop after multiple consecutive days of AQI 300+**

The synchronized decline across Andheri East, Malad, and Borivali East indicated that immediate GRAP-4 restrictions — particularly construction halts, dust suppression measures, and traffic controls — helped **break the continuous high-pollution cycle** and allowed the city's air to recover.

## 6. Mumbai vs. Delhi: Key GRAP Differences

| Aspect | Mumbai GRAP | Delhi GRAP (CAQM) |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| Governing Authority | BMC (Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation) | CAQM (Commission for Air Quality Management) |
| GRAP Stage triggered | Stage 4 at AQI 200–300 (Very Poor) | Stage 4 at AQI 450+ (Severe+) |
| Trigger AQI level | Lower threshold; activated for neighbourhood-level spikes | Citywide AQI must hit Severe+ for Stage IV |
| Vehicle restrictions | No BS-III/BS-IV vehicle ban applied | BS-III petrol and BS-IV diesel cars restricted in NCR |
| Truck entry | No truck entry ban in Mumbai GRAP Stage 4 | Entry of truck traffic barred during GRAP IV |
| Office operations | Not specified in Mumbai GRAP | Government offices at 50% capacity; work-from-home advised for private firms |
| Scope | Applied to specific neighbourhoods with poor AQI | Applied across the entire NCR region |

## 7. Pollution Pattern Analysis — Key Observations

1. **Mumbai's winter pollution is now mimicking Delhi-NCR patterns** — multi-day sustained AQI episodes above 300 are occurring
2. **Sea breeze protection is diminishing** due to:
   - Rising construction of high-rise buildings blocking coastal wind penetration
   - Increased emission sources (construction, traffic, waste burning)
3. **Temperature inversion effect is present in Mumbai's suburbs** — pollutants trapped near ground especially at night
4. **Pollution is not uniform across the city** — coastal areas experience less pollution while inland suburbs (Malad, Borivali, Andheri East, Mulund) face higher pollution
5. Consistent multi-day AQI above 200–300 in these suburbs qualifies as an **emergency phase** requiring immediate GRAP response

---

## 8. Authority Decision Support — GRAP Activation Checklist

**Conditions warranting GRAP Stage 4 activation in Mumbai:**
- AQI at multiple stations crosses 300 (Very Poor) for multiple consecutive hours
- Morning and evening pollution peaks are synchronized across 2+ suburbs
- Overnight AQI stays above 200 without dispersion
- Temperature inversion or stagnant wind conditions prevail
- Pollution episode persists for 3+ days without recovery to Moderate levels

**Immediate actions post-activation:**
- Notify all ward engineers for site inspections
- Deploy mechanised sweeping equipment citywide
- Activate traffic management for congestion control
- Issue construction site compliance notices
- Conduct PUC checking drives
- Monitor AQI hourly at affected station clusters
