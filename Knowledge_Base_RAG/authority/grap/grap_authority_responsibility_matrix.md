# GRAP Authority Responsibility Matrix: Maharashtra / Mumbai

**Source:** Maharashtra GRAP Official Framework (NGT Order 2019) + Mumbai Implementation Records  
**Category:** Authority — GRAP Responsibility Reference  

---

## Purpose

This document provides a structured, authority-centric mapping of GRAP responsibilities. It is designed to answer:
- Which authority is responsible for a specific action?
- What does each authority do at each GRAP stage?
- In what situations does a specific authority become the lead actor?

All authority assignments are sourced directly from the Maharashtra GRAP official framework.

---

## 1. MPCB — Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (Regional / Sub-Regional Officer)

### Designation in GRAP
"Concerned Regional Officer / Sub-Regional Officer, Maharashtra Pollution Control Board"

### Primary Mandate
Environmental enforcement, industrial emission control, ambient air quality monitoring, and DG set prohibition.

### Responsibilities by GRAP Category

| GRAP Category | MPCB Actions |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Category I (Moderate to Poor)** | Enforce pollution control regulations in all industries within the city. Issue notices to non-compliant industrial units. Enforce and support dust control compliance at construction sites (jointly with Municipal Corporation). Disseminate public information about pollution levels. |
| **Category II (Very Poor)** | Stop/prohibit use of diesel generator sets (DG sets) within city limits. Conduct surprise inspections of commercial and industrial DG set users. Issue violation notices and seal non-compliant DG sets. Issue public health advisory in coordination with Municipal Corporation through media channels. |
| **Category III (Severe)** | Issue closure orders to major polluting industrial units: brick kilns, hot mix plants, stone crushers, thermal power plants, and other major emission sources identified in emission inventory. Enforce closures physically; seal non-compliant units under EP Act and Air Act authority. |
| **Category IV (Emergency)** | Enforce and support all Category I–III actions with heightened intensity. Support Municipal Corporation in halting all construction activities (joint jurisdiction). Coordinate with Task Force on additional emergency measures. |

### Situations Where MPCB Becomes Lead Authority
- Any action involving **industrial unit compliance or closure**
- Any action involving **DG set prohibition enforcement**
- **Ambient air quality monitoring** and threshold assessment
- Construction site environmental non-compliance (jointly)
- Port and marine activity emission violations (jointly with Port Authority)
- Issuing **public health advisories** about pollution (jointly with Municipal Corporation)

### Tools and Legal Powers
- Closure orders under the Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981
- Directions under Environment Protection Act, 1986
- Online Continuous Emission Monitoring System (OCEMS) data access for industries
- Ambient Air Quality Monitoring Station (CAAQMS) network management
- Penalty proceedings against polluting industries

## 2. Municipal Corporation (BMC / Concerned Municipal Corporation or Council)

### Designation in GRAP
"Concerned Municipal Corporation / Council"  
In Mumbai: **Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC)**

### Primary Mandate
Urban enforcement, dust control, solid waste management, road maintenance, public transport coordination, parking management, and public communications.

### Responsibilities by GRAP Category

| GRAP Category | Municipal Corporation Actions |
|---------------|------------------------------|
| **Category I (Moderate to Poor)** | Enforce and stop garbage burning in landfills and public spaces; levy heavy fines on violators. Enforce dust control rules at construction sites; close non-compliant sites. Do periodic mechanized sweeping on high-traffic roads and water sprinkling on paved and unpaved roads. Disseminate public information via social media, apps, and control room contact details. |
| **Category II (Very Poor)** | Enhance parking fees by 3–4 times at all municipal parking facilities. Augment bus services by deploying contract buses and increasing frequency. Augment metro services in coordination with Metro Rail Corporation (increase frequency, reduce headways). Introduce differential pricing for off-peak travel on public transport. Issue public health advisory through media channels jointly with MPCB. |
| **Category III (Severe)** | Intensify public transport services beyond Category II augmentation (emergency bus fleet deployment). Further increase frequency of mechanized road cleaning — move from periodic to daily or multiple-times-daily. |
| **Category IV (Emergency)** | Halt all construction activities within municipal limits (joint order with MPCB). Coordinate with Traffic Police to implement truck/heavy vehicle restriction (municipal notification). Flying squad deployment: BMC engineers + Traffic Police for ward-level compliance inspection. |

### Situations Where Municipal Corporation Becomes Lead Authority
- **Garbage and waste burning enforcement** — primary lead at all GRAP stages
- **Mechanized road sweeping and water sprinkling** — sole operational authority
- **Parking fee enhancement** — sole authority over municipal parking
- **Construction site dust enforcement** (shared with MPCB; BMC controls building permission stop-work)
- **Public information dissemination** — primary channel for citizen-facing communications
- **Bus service augmentation** — through BEST and contract procurement
- **Halting construction under Category IV** — joint authority with MPCB

### Tools and Legal Powers
- Building plan stop-work notices (BMC)
- Solid Waste Management bylaws for garbage burning penalties
- Municipal Corporation Act provisions for nuisance abatement
- Control over BEST bus fleet operations
- Municipal parking lot management authority
- BMC 1916 helpline and complaint management system (Aaple Sarkar portal)

## 3. Traffic Police (Concerned Traffic Police)

### Designation in GRAP
"Concerned Traffic Police"

### Primary Mandate
Traffic flow management, vehicle emission enforcement, and vehicle restriction operations.

### Responsibilities by GRAP Category

| GRAP Category | Traffic Police Actions |
|---------------|-----------------------|
| **Category I (Moderate to Poor)** | Deploy traffic police personnel at identified vulnerable traffic congestion points and intersections to ensure smooth traffic flow and reduce idling emissions. |
| **Category II (Very Poor)** | Continue Category I deployments. Coordinate with RTO on PUC enforcement drives. Support DG set enforcement logistics (if raids require traffic support). |
| **Category III (Severe)** | Continue Category I–II actions. Support implementation of enhanced transport measures. |
| **Category IV (Emergency)** | Establish vehicle entry checkpoints at all city entry points to stop trucks and heavy vehicles from entering the city. Enforce odd-even scheme for private vehicles based on license plate number (challaning non-compliant vehicles). Deploy joint enforcement teams with RTO for vehicle-related restrictions. Participate in BMC flying squads for ward-level GRAP compliance inspection. |

### Situations Where Traffic Police Become Lead Authority
- **Category I:** Any situation involving traffic congestion driving localized pollution (traffic police is lead at all stages for traffic management)
- **Category IV:** Lead authority for truck/heavy vehicle restriction at city entry points
- **Category IV:** Lead enforcement authority for odd-even vehicle scheme
- **Any category:** Vehicle-related emission hotspot management (e.g., idling at signals, high-congestion corridors)

### Tools and Legal Powers
- Motor Vehicles Act — challaning authority for traffic violations
- Authority to set up checkpoints and vehicle interception at any point
- Coordination with National Highways Authority for entry point management
- Joint authority with RTO for vehicle fitness and emission checks

## 4. RTO — Regional Transport Office

### Designation in GRAP
"Concerned RTO"

### Primary Mandate
Vehicle registration, fitness certification, PUC (Pollution Under Control) certificate enforcement, and vehicle compliance management.

### Responsibilities by GRAP Category

| GRAP Category | RTO Actions |
|---------------|------------|
| **Category I (Moderate to Poor)** | Implement strict vigilance and enforcement of PUC norms. Conduct joint PUC checking drives with Traffic Police. Challan vehicles without valid PUC certificates. |
| **Category II (Very Poor)** | Continue and intensify PUC enforcement. Identify and flag commercial vehicles (autos, taxis, trucks) operating without valid PUC. |
| **Category III (Severe)** | Continue PUC enforcement. |
| **Category IV (Emergency)** | Support Traffic Police in enforcing odd-even scheme (joint challaning authority for vehicle number plate violations). Enforce truck and heavy vehicle restrictions at entry checkpoints (jointly with Traffic Police). |

### Situations Where RTO Becomes Lead Authority
- **PUC certification enforcement** — lead authority for checking vehicle emission compliance documents
- **Vehicle fitness enforcement** — vehicles identified as major polluters referred to RTO for fitness cancellation
- **Category IV:** Co-lead with Traffic Police for vehicle scheme enforcement (odd-even, truck ban)

### Tools and Legal Powers
- Central Motor Vehicles Rules — authority for PUC certificate mandate enforcement
- Vehicle fitness certificate authority — can suspend registration of non-compliant vehicles
- Motor Vehicles Act — penalty authority for PUC violations

## 5. Transport Department (Concerned Transport Department)

### Designation in GRAP
"Concerned Municipal Corporation / Council, Transport Department"

### Primary Mandate
Coordination and policy oversight for public transport augmentation, route planning, and fare structure modifications.

### Responsibilities by GRAP Category

| GRAP Category | Transport Department Actions |
|---------------|------------------------------|
| **Category I (Moderate to Poor)** | No specific GRAP-mandated actions (baseline monitoring and readiness). |
| **Category II (Very Poor)** | Coordinate augmentation of bus services — mobilise contract buses to supplement BEST fleet. Facilitate and approve introduction of differential (off-peak) transport pricing. Coordinate with Municipal Corporation on transport demand management. |
| **Category III (Severe)** | Further intensify bus service augmentation beyond Category II. Emergency route adjustments to maximise public transport capacity on high-demand corridors. |
| **Category IV (Emergency)** | Coordinate full public transport emergency response: maximum bus fleet deployment, emergency procurement of additional contract vehicles if needed. |

### Situations Where Transport Department Becomes Lead Authority
- All situations involving **bus fleet augmentation policy** and **contract bus procurement**
- **Differential fare structure modification** — requires Transport Department approval
- **Emergency transport demand management** during Category IV

## 6. Metro Rail Corporation (Mumbai Metro / MMRCL / MMRDA)

### Designation in GRAP
"Concerned Municipal Corporation / Council, Metro Rail Corporation"

### Primary Mandate
Metro train operations, frequency management, and passenger capacity optimization during high-demand GRAP periods.

### Responsibilities by GRAP Category

| GRAP Category | Metro Rail Corporation Actions |
|---------------|-------------------------------|
| **Category I (Moderate to Poor)** | No specific mandated action; standard operations. |
| **Category II (Very Poor)** | Increase metro service frequency on all operational lines. Reduce headways (time gap between trains) during peak hours. Augment crowd management at high-volume stations. |
| **Category III (Severe)** | Further intensify metro frequency increase beyond Category II levels. Coordinate with BMC and Transport Department on integrated transport demand surge management. |
| **Category IV (Emergency)** | Maximum frequency operation on all metro lines. Coordinate with BMC on bus feeder services to metro stations to facilitate modal shift from private vehicles restricted under odd-even scheme. |

### Situations Where Metro Rail Corporation Becomes Lead Authority
- Any GRAP action involving **metro service frequency increases** or **passenger capacity expansion**
- Coordination with BMC and Transport Department on **modal shift strategy** during Category II–IV

### Mumbai Metro Operational Lines (as of 2025)
- Metro Line 1: Versova – Andheri – Ghatkopar (DMRC-operated)
- Metro Line 2A: Dahisar – D.N. Nagar
- Metro Line 7: Andheri East – Dahisar East
- Metro Line 2B: D.N. Nagar – BKC (operational status to be confirmed)
- Additional lines under construction — phased operational integration

## 7. Task Force Committee (City-Level Implementation Committee)

### Designation in GRAP
"Members of Task Force Committee appointed vide Government Resolution dated 18/09/2019 of City-Level Implementation Committee"

### Composition
The City-Level Implementation Committee constituted under NCAP (Government Resolution No. NCA 2018/CR.196(2)/T.C.2, dated 18/09/2019). Typically includes:
- Municipal Commissioner (or nominee) — Chairperson
- MPCB Regional Officer — Member
- Police Commissioner nominee (Traffic) — Member
- RTO — Member
- Transport Department representative — Member
- Metro Rail Corporation representative — Member
- Health Department representative — Member
- Other domain experts as co-opted by the Committee

### Primary Mandate
Overall governance of GRAP implementation; GRAP stage invocation and de-escalation authority; authority to impose measures beyond the standard GRAP schedule; coordination and dispute resolution between implementing agencies.

### Responsibilities by GRAP Category

| GRAP Category | Task Force Actions |
|---------------|--------------------|
| **Category I–III** | Monitor GRAP implementation progress. Receive daily enforcement reports from all implementing agencies. Convene review meetings to assess compliance gaps. Issue corrective directions to implementing agencies. Approve pre-emptive stage escalation based on SAFAR forecast data. |
| **Category IV (Emergency)** | **Sole authority** to decide on additional emergency measures beyond the standard Category IV schedule. This includes shutting of specific activities, institutes, offices, schools, commercial establishments, or any other category not explicitly listed in the GRAP schedule. Convene emergency meeting within 24 hours of Category IV trigger. Issue emergency orders with immediate effect. Approve or deny exemptions from Emergency restrictions (e.g., hospital exemptions from construction halt, essential vehicle exemptions from truck ban). |

### Situations Where Task Force Becomes Lead Authority
- **All GRAP stage invocation decisions** — no stage can be activated without Task Force order
- **All de-escalation decisions** — no stage can be revoked without Task Force approval
- **Category IV Emergency additional measures** — sole authority for emergency decisions beyond the GRAP schedule
- **Exemption approvals** — any request for exemption from mandatory restrictions (hospital exemptions, essential commodity vehicle permits, critical infrastructure construction continuity)
- **Modifications to GRAP** — authority to revise the GRAP schedule, add measures, or make exceptions based on ground conditions
- **Inter-agency conflict resolution** — when implementing agencies have jurisdictional disputes

### Task Force Tools and Legal Powers
- Government Resolution authority for issuing binding orders to all city-level agencies
- Can direct all municipal departments, police, and transport bodies simultaneously
- Authority to recommend escalation to State-level Emergency (if city-level measures are insufficient)
- GRAP orders issued by Task Force have legal enforceability under NGT mandate
