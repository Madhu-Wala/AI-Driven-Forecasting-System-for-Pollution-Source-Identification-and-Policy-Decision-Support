## 1. Mumbai GRAP Stage 4 Activation Context

**Authority:** Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC)  
**Trigger event:** Sharp dip in air quality across multiple Mumbai suburbs, with some areas recording 'severe' (AQI 400+) pollution levels

The BMC triggered GRAP Stage IV despite Mumbai's pollution being comparatively lower than Delhi's (where AQI hovered around 300–400), because the **neighbourhood-level AQI spikes in specific wards crossed 'Very Poor' to 'Severe' thresholds**.

**CPCB's citywide AQI for Mumbai on the Monday following activation:** AQI 111 (Moderate), showing the localized nature of the pollution — specific neighbourhoods were in severe trouble even as the city average remained moderate.

Pollution in affected areas was driven primarily by **PM₂.₅ and PM₁₀** from:
- Construction dust
- Waste burning
- Traffic congestion in dense suburban corridors

## 2. BMC GRAP Stage 4 Enforcement Operations

### Flying Squads Deployed
Special flying squads comprising:
- Engineers
- Police teams
- GPS-enabled vehicles

These squads are **inspecting every ward** to enforce pollution norms.

**Inspection findings:**
- Out of **70 sites checked**, **53 were found violating dust-control rules**
- Violating sites issued notices; repeat violators face closure orders

### Key Enforcement Actions
- Garbage-burning bans strictly enforced in landfills and public spaces
- Construction site dust control audits with on-the-spot notices
- Industrial units violating emission norms ordered to close
- Traffic management deployed to reduce congestion-linked emission spikes
- Vehicle PUC checks intensified across the city

## 3. Comparison: Mumbai GRAP Stage 4 vs. Delhi GRAP Stage IV

### Trigger Threshold Differences
- **Mumbai GRAP Stage 4**: Activated when neighbourhood-level AQI hits 'Very Poor' (AQI 200–300) or above
- **Delhi GRAP Stage IV (CAQM)**: Activated when citywide AQI crosses 450 (Severe+)

This means Mumbai's GRAP is more **localized and responsive** to neighbourhood-level pollution events, while Delhi's GRAP responds to macro-level, citywide emergencies.

### Restriction Differences

| Restriction | Mumbai GRAP Stage 4 | Delhi GRAP Stage IV |
|-------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Construction halt | Dust control enforced; partial halt | Major construction halted |
| Vehicle restrictions | PUC checks intensified | BS-III petrol and BS-IV diesel banned in NCR |
| Truck traffic | No blanket ban | Entry of trucks barred |
| Work-from-home | Not mandated | Private firms advised to implement WFH |
| Government offices | Normal operations | Operate at 50% capacity |
| Industrial units | Violating units closed | Stricter closures |

### Why Mumbai's Measures Differ
1. **Different governance structure**: Mumbai's air quality management is led by BMC, not a regional body like CAQM
2. **Lower baseline pollution**: Mumbai's coastal geography provides natural dispersion, requiring emergency responses at lower absolute AQI thresholds
3. **Different major pollution sources**: Mumbai's key sources are construction, waste burning, and port activities — not large-scale crop burning
4. **Neighbourhood heterogeneity**: Mumbai's pollution is highly localized — targeted responses to specific wards are more effective than blanket city-wide bans

## 4. Policy Implications for Mumbai Air Quality Management

### Positive Lessons
- **Neighbourhood-level GRAP activation** is more responsive than waiting for citywide AQI thresholds
- **Flying squad model with GPS-enabled enforcement** is effective for rapid site inspection
- GRAP Stage 4 activation in November 2025 resulted in measurable AQI improvement within hours of implementation

### Gaps Identified
- Mumbai lacks a **comprehensive multi-stage GRAP framework** comparable to Delhi's four-stage CAQM system
- No standardized **pre-GRAP advisory system** for citizens during the buildup phase (AQI 100–200)
- Construction site compliance is insufficient — 53 of 70 sites inspected violated dust-control norms
- Absence of binding **vehicle restriction orders** limits traffic-related emission reductions

### Recommended Enhancements for Mumbai's GRAP
1. Establish a formal **multi-stage GRAP framework** with clear AQI thresholds for each stage
2. Create a **24-hour AQI monitoring and alert system** that automatically notifies BMC ward officers when thresholds are crossed
3. Implement **pre-declared construction shutdown protocols** for periods of predicted weather stagnation
4. Introduce **emission-based vehicle access restrictions** for the most polluted wards
5. Coordinate with Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB) for industrial unit monitoring during GRAP activation
