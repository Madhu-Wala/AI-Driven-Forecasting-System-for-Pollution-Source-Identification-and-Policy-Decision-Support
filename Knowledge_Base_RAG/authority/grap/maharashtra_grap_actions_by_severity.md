# Maharashtra GRAP: Complete Actions by Severity Category

**Source:** Maharashtra Graded Response Action Plan — Official Action Schedule  
**Legal Mandate:** NGT Order dated 20/11/2019 | Government Resolution No. NCA 2018/CR.196(2)/T.C.2, dated 18/09/2019  
**Category:** Authority — GRAP Actionable Reference  

---

## Critical Operational Rules (Apply to All Categories)

- **All actions are cumulative.** When a higher category is activated, all actions from lower categories remain in force.
- **Minimum 15-day activation window.** Once a stage is invoked, its actions continue for at least 15 days or until AQI returns below the threshold.
- **Pre-emptive activation is permitted.** Task Force may invoke the next stage based on SAFAR/IITM forecast projections before the threshold is actually crossed.
- **Actions are mandatory, not advisory,** once the Task Force invokes the corresponding GRAP stage.

---

## CATEGORY I — Moderate to Poor

**PM₂.₅ Trigger:** 61 – 120 µg/m³  
**PM₁₀ Trigger:** 101 – 350 µg/m³  
**AQI Range (approx.):** Moderate to Poor (~101–300)

This is the baseline enforcement stage. Focus is on **source control, dust management, traffic flow, and public information**. All actions below are activated simultaneously when Category I is invoked.

---

### Action 1: Garbage Burning Enforcement
**Action:** Stringently enforce and stop garbage burning in landfills and all public places. Impose heavy fines on persons found responsible.  
**Responsible Authority:** Concerned Municipal Corporation  
**Scope:** All landfill sites, open areas, roadsides, and public spaces within municipal limits  
**Enforcement mechanism:** Municipal enforcement squads conduct site inspections; fines levied on violators  

---

### Action 2: Construction Site Dust Control
**Action:** Stringently enforce dust control rules at all active construction sites. Close sites found to be non-compliant with dust mitigation norms.  
**Responsible Authority:** Concerned Regional Officer / Sub-Regional Officer, Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB); Concerned Municipal Corporation  
**Scope:** All construction and demolition sites, road-laying projects, excavation works  
**Non-compliance consequence:** Immediate site closure order  
**Dust control norms include:** covering stockpiles with tarpaulin, wheel-washing at site exits, perimeter hoarding, water sprinkling on active excavation and demolition, use of anti-smog guns at large sites (>20,000 sq.m.)  

---

### Action 3: Industrial Pollution Control Enforcement
**Action:** Stringently enforce pollution control regulations in all industries. Non-compliant industrial units to be penalised.  
**Responsible Authority:** Concerned Regional Officer / Sub-Regional Officer, Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB)  
**Scope:** All industrial units within the city and its industrial zones  
**Enforcement includes:** Inspection of stack emission compliance, OCEMS (Online Continuous Emission Monitoring System) data verification, identification and action against units exceeding prescribed emission limits  

---

### Action 4: Traffic Deployment for Congestion Management
**Action:** Deploy traffic police at identified vulnerable traffic intersections and corridors to ensure smooth traffic flow and minimize idling emissions.  
**Responsible Authority:** Concerned Traffic Police  
**Scope:** High-congestion traffic junctions, arterial roads, areas with known traffic-linked pollution hotspots  
**Rationale:** Idling vehicles at congested intersections produce disproportionately high localized PM₂.₅ and NOx emissions; smooth flow reduces per-kilometer emission factor  

---

### Action 5: PUC (Pollution Under Control) Enforcement
**Action:** Implement strict vigilance and enforcement of PUC norms. Intercept and penalize vehicles without valid PUC certificates.  
**Responsible Authority:** Concerned RTO (Regional Transport Office); Traffic Police  
**Scope:** All roads, including spot-checking at entry points, fuel stations, and high-traffic corridors  
**Penalty:** Vehicles found operating without valid PUC are chalaned; repeat offenders face registration suspension  

---

### Action 6: Mechanized Road Sweeping and Water Sprinkling
**Action:** Conduct periodic mechanized sweeping on high-traffic roads. Perform water sprinkling on both paved and unpaved roads to suppress road dust.  
**Responsible Authority:** Concerned Municipal Corporation  
**Scope:** All major roads, secondary roads, and identified unpaved stretches within municipal limits  
**Frequency:** Periodic during Category I; frequency increases in higher categories  
**Special focus:** Unpaved roads and roads adjacent to construction sites where re-suspension of road dust is highest  

---

### Action 7: Public Information Dissemination
**Action:** Use social media platforms and mobile apps to:
- Inform citizens about current pollution levels
- Provide contact details of the pollution control room
- Enable citizens to report polluting activities and sources to concerned authorities
- Communicate what actions the government is taking based on the current level of pollution  

**Responsible Authority:** Concerned Municipal Corporation; MPCB  
**Channels:** Official social media handles (Twitter/X, Facebook, Instagram), WhatsApp broadcast lists, dedicated AQI apps (SAFAR, MPCB portal), SMS alerts, local news channels  
**Purpose:** Public awareness + crowd-sourced enforcement reporting — citizens become active participants in identifying violations

## CATEGORY II — Very Poor

**PM₂.₅ Trigger:** 121 – 250 µg/m³  
**PM₁₀ Trigger:** 351 – 430 µg/m³  
**AQI Range (approx.):** Very Poor (~301–400)

**All Category I actions remain active.** Category II adds demand-side management measures — reducing pollutant-generating activity through economic disincentives and augmenting clean transport alternatives.

---

### Action 1: Diesel Generator Set Prohibition
**Action:** Stop the use of diesel generator sets (DG sets) within the city limits.  
**Responsible Authority:** Concerned Regional Officer / Sub-Regional Officer, Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB)  
**Scope:** All commercial, industrial, and residential DG sets; exception may be permitted for hospitals, emergency services, and data centers at Task Force discretion  
**Enforcement:** MPCB inspectors conduct surprise raids; violating units face shutdown orders and penalty under Environment Protection Act  
**Rationale:** DG sets run on high-sulfur diesel and emit significant PM₂.₅, NOx, and SO₂; prohibition during Very Poor AQI reduces a significant localized emission source  

---

### Action 2: Parking Fee Enhancement
**Action:** Enhance parking fees by 3 to 4 times the standard rate across all municipal parking facilities.  
**Responsible Authority:** Concerned Municipal Corporation / Council  
**Scope:** All municipal parking lots, on-street paid parking zones, and commercial parking areas under municipal jurisdiction  
**Rationale:** Higher parking costs disincentivize private vehicle use during poor air quality periods; drives modal shift toward public transport  
**Implementation:** Municipal Commissioner issues order to parking contractors and municipal parking staff; signage updated to reflect revised rates  

---

### Action 3: Augment Bus and Metro Services
**Action:** Increase the frequency and capacity of bus and metro services by augmenting contract buses and increasing service frequency. Ensure adequate availability of public transport to absorb modal shift from private vehicles.  
**Responsible Authorities:**  
- Bus augmentation: Concerned Municipal Corporation / Council + Transport Department  
- Metro frequency increase: Concerned Municipal Corporation / Council + Metro Rail Corporation (Mumbai Metro / MMRDA)  
**Scope:** All existing bus and metro routes within the city; additional focus on routes connecting high-density residential areas to commercial/employment zones  
**Specific measures:**
  - Contract buses deployed to supplement BEST / MSRTC fleet
  - Increased metro frequency on all operational lines
  - Reduce headways (time between consecutive services) during peak hours

---

### Action 4: Introduce Differential Transport Rates for Off-Peak Travel
**Action:** Introduce differential pricing/rates to encourage commuters to travel during off-peak hours, reducing peak-hour congestion and associated emissions.  
**Responsible Authority:** Concerned Municipal Corporation / Council; Transport Department  
**Scope:** Public transport (bus, metro, suburban rail where applicable) fare structures  
**Rationale:** Spreading travel demand across hours reduces congestion peaks which are associated with the highest vehicular emission rates  

---

### Action 5: Public Health Advisory — Respiratory and Cardiac Patients
**Action:** Issue public health alerts through newspapers, TV, and radio channels advising:
- People with respiratory conditions (asthma, COPD, bronchitis) to avoid polluted areas
- People with cardiac conditions to restrict outdoor movement
- All citizens to limit unnecessary outdoor exposure during Very Poor AQI episodes  

**Responsible Authority:** Concerned Regional Officer / Sub-Regional Officer, MPCB; Concerned Municipal Corporation / Council  
**Channels:** Print media (newspapers), broadcast media (TV/radio), social media (continued from Category I), hospital advisory systems  
**Target audience:** Primarily vulnerable populations — respiratory and cardiac patients, elderly, children; general population advisory also issued  

---

## CATEGORY III — Severe

**PM₂.₅ Trigger:** Above 250 µg/m³  
**PM₁₀ Trigger:** Above 430 µg/m³  
**AQI Range (approx.):** Severe (~401–450)

**All Category I and Category II actions remain active.** Category III adds major industrial shutdowns and further intensification of transport measures.

---

### Action 1: Closure of Major Polluting Industrial Units
**Action:** Close down all major polluting industrial units, specifically including:
- **Brick kilns**
- **Hot mix plants**
- **Stone crushers**
- **Thermal power plants**
- Any other major emission-generating industrial establishments as identified by MPCB  

**Responsible Authority:** Concerned Regional Officer / Sub-Regional Officer, Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB)  
**Scope:** All such units within the city and its surrounding industrial zones that contribute to ambient PM₂.₅ and PM₁₀  
**Legal authority:** MPCB Regional Officers have authority to issue closure orders under the Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981 and Environment Protection Act, 1986  
**Enforcement:** Closure orders issued in writing; MPCB inspectors seal units if owners fail to comply; penalties for non-compliance include legal action and cancellation of consent-to-operate  

---

### Action 2: Intensify Public Transport Services
**Action:** Further intensify public transport services beyond the Category II augmentation. Introduce differential rates specifically to encourage off-peak travel.  
**Responsible Authority:** Concerned Municipal Corporation / Council; Transport Department  
**Scope:** All public transport modes in the city — buses, metro, suburban rail  
**Distinction from Category II:** Category III intensification goes beyond adding contract buses; may include emergency fleet deployment, suspension of non-essential routes in favour of high-demand corridors, and mandatory transport operator compliance orders  

---

### Action 3: Increase Frequency of Mechanized Road Cleaning
**Action:** Increase the frequency of mechanized road cleaning and water sprinkling on roads — beyond the periodic schedule mandated under Category I.  
**Responsible Authority:** Concerned Municipal Corporation / Council  
**Scope:** All major roads and secondary roads; intensified focus on identified high-dust corridors  
**Frequency guidance:** Under Severe AQI, sweeping and sprinkling should move from periodic to daily or multiple-times-daily on high-traffic roads  

---

## CATEGORY IV — Emergency / Severe+

**PM₂.₅ Trigger:** 300 µg/m³ or above, persisting for **48 continuous hours or more**  
**PM₁₀ Trigger:** 500 µg/m³ or above, persisting for **48 continuous hours or more**  
**AQI Range (approx.):** Emergency / Severe+ (AQI >450)

**All Category I, II, and III actions remain active.** Category IV imposes the hardest restrictions on mobility and activities. Duration of sustained pollution (48-hour persistence) is the critical trigger distinguishing this from Category III.

---

### Action 1: Stop Entry of Trucks and Heavy Vehicles
**Action:** Stop the entry of all trucks and heavy vehicles into the city. No heavy commercial vehicles to be permitted entry except for essential services.  
**Responsible Authority:** Concerned Traffic Police; Regional Transport Office (RTO)  
**Scope:** All entry points to the city — all major arterial roads, expressway exits, and toll points leading into the city  
**Exemptions (at Task Force discretion):** Vehicles carrying essential commodities (food, medicines, fuel for hospitals), garbage collection vehicles, emergency services  
**Implementation:** Checkpoints established at all major city entry points; police personnel deployed 24×7; vehicle turnaround points designated for non-exempt vehicles  

---

### Action 2: Stop All Construction Activities
**Action:** Halt all construction activities within the city limits — including building construction, road construction, demolition, and infrastructure works.  
**Responsible Authority:** Concerned Municipal Corporation; Concerned Regional Officer, MPCB  
**Scope:** All construction and demolition sites without exception; includes public sector projects (road widening, metro construction) and private sector developments  
**Exceptions (at Task Force discretion):** Emergency repair work related to water/sewage/power supply; hospital construction in critical phase  
**Enforcement:** Joint teams of municipal officers and MPCB inspectors; sealed sites; daily inspection rounds  

---

### Action 3: Introduce Odd-Even Scheme for Private Vehicles
**Action:** Implement the odd-even scheme for private vehicles based on license plate number. Minimize exemptions to maximize effectiveness.  
**Responsible Authority:** Concerned Traffic Police; Regional Transport Office (RTO)  
**Scope:** All private four-wheelers within city limits  
**Mechanism:**
  - Odd-numbered license plates permitted on odd calendar dates
  - Even-numbered license plates permitted on even calendar dates
  - Two-wheelers, buses, ambulances, government vehicles may be considered for exemption at Task Force discretion, though exemptions should be minimized
  - Enforcement at key traffic intersections, arterial roads, and CBD entry points  
**Rationale:** Reduces private vehicle trips by approximately 50%, significantly cutting vehicular PM₂.₅ and NOx emissions during emergency pollution episodes  

---

### Action 4: Task Force Emergency Decisions
**Action:** The Task Force Committee convenes to evaluate the situation and take decisions on any **additional steps** beyond the standard Category IV schedule, including but not limited to:
- Shutting of specific activities or categories of activities not covered in the standard schedule
- Shutting of specific institutes, offices, schools, or commercial establishments
- Imposing temporary work-from-home mandates for government and private sector offices
- Restricting public gatherings or outdoor events
- Any other extraordinary measure deemed necessary for public health protection  

**Responsible Authority:** Members of the Task Force Committee appointed under Government Resolution dated 18/09/2019 of the City-Level Implementation Committee  
**Process:** Emergency meeting convened within 24 hours of Category IV trigger; decisions taken collectively; orders issued with immediate effect  
**Scope:** Unlimited — Task Force has authority to impose measures beyond the GRAP schedule based on ground conditions and forecast

## AQI-to-Action Quick Reference for Authority Agents

**Query: AQI is 280 (PM₂.₅ ~115 µg/m³). What actions are required?**  
→ PM₂.₅ 115 µg/m³ falls in Category I (61–120 µg/m³). Activate all 7 Category I actions. Authorities: Municipal Corporation, MPCB, Traffic Police, RTO.

**Query: AQI is 350 (PM₂.₅ ~160 µg/m³). What actions are required?**  
→ PM₂.₅ 160 µg/m³ falls in Category II (121–250 µg/m³). Activate all Category I + all 5 Category II actions. DG sets banned; parking fees tripled/quadrupled; buses and metro augmented.

**Query: AQI is 420 (PM₂.₅ ~270 µg/m³). What actions are required?**  
→ PM₂.₅ 270 µg/m³ falls in Category III (>250 µg/m³). Activate all Category I + II + 3 Category III actions. Brick kilns, hot mix plants, stone crushers, thermal power plants must be shut down.

**Query: AQI has been above 450 for 3 consecutive days (PM₂.₅ persistently >300 µg/m³). What actions are required?**  
→ 48-hour persistence criterion met. Activate Category IV (Emergency). All prior category actions plus: stop trucks entering city; halt all construction; implement odd-even vehicle scheme; convene Task Force for additional emergency measures.
