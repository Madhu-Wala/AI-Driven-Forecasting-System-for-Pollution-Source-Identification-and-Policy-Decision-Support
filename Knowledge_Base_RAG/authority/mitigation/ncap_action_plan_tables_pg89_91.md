## Summary: NCAP GRAP Emergency Response Requirement (All 102 NACs)

As part of NCAP, all 102 non-attainment cities are required to develop and operationalize:
1. **Emergency Response System (ERS) / Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP)** — AQI threshold-based mandatory action plan
2. **Public Grievance Redressal Portal (PGRP) / helpline** — for citizen pollution reporting
3. **City-Level Implementation Committee** — oversight and GRAP invocation authority
4. **Performance tracking on PRANA portal** — quarterly progress reports to CPCB

**NCAP Performance-Based Fund Release:**
- First instalment: **60% of allocated NCAP funds** released upon submission of utilization certificate and city action plans.
- Second instalment: **40% released** based on performance assessment against PM reduction targets.
- Cities assessed annually; performance data submitted on PRANA portal.
