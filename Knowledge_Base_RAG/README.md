# Air Quality RAG Knowledge Base

## Overview

This repository contains the knowledge base used by the Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) module of the **AI-Driven Air Quality Monitoring, Forecasting, and Policy Decision Support System**.

The knowledge base provides domain-specific information for generating reliable responses related to:

- Citizen health advisories
- Air Quality Index (AQI) interpretation
- Pollution mitigation strategies
- Government policies and regulations
- GRAP (Graded Response Action Plan)
- NCAP (National Clean Air Programme)
- CPCB air quality standards

The RAG pipeline retrieves relevant documents from this knowledge base before passing them to the Large Language Model (LLM), enabling grounded and context-aware responses.

## Folder Description

### citizen/

Contains documents intended for public-facing recommendations.

Includes:

- Health precautions
- AQI interpretation
- Exposure reduction measures
- Outdoor activity guidance
- Mask recommendations
- Indoor air quality recommendations

---

### authority/

Contains policy and regulatory documents for decision support.

Includes:

- NCAP mitigation strategies
- CPCB standards
- Maharashtra GRAP framework
- Government action plans
- Pollution control measures
- Sector-wise mitigation recommendations

## Knowledge Sources

The documents were compiled from authoritative and publicly available sources including:

- Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)
- National Clean Air Programme (NCAP)
- Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB)
- Government of India (PIB)
- Official GRAP documentation
- Reputed healthcare organizations for citizen health advisories

---

## Purpose

This knowledge base supports two major modules:

### Citizen Advisory

Generates context-aware recommendations such as:

- Outdoor activity planning
- Health precautions
- Mask recommendations
- Air purifier suggestions
- AQI explanation

### Policy Decision Support

Generates recommendations for authorities such as:

- GRAP actions
- Pollution mitigation strategies
- Sector-specific interventions
- Policy references
- Regulatory guidance

---

## Notes

- Documents are stored in Markdown format for efficient parsing and embedding generation.
- Information has been organized by topic to improve retrieval accuracy.
- Responses generated by the RAG system are grounded in the retrieved documents rather than relying solely on the LLM's internal knowledge.
